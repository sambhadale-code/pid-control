# Teaching Notes

- Teach from physical intuition first: a controller reduces error like adjusting shower temperature.
- Keep the assignment anchored to the required 10 sections, but present them as a narrative, not as a dry checklist.
- Prefer simple, reproducible code with NumPy and Matplotlib over flashy examples.
- When the user asks for explanations, always connect the math to a graph or a simple real-world example.
- Ask follow-up questions whenever a tuning result or section is unclear; the goal is deep understanding, not just finishing the report.
